## Fixes

- **Hover tooltips in the glass panel were hard to read over a list.** [#399](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/399) — relates to [#398](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/issues/398)

  On macOS 27 the tooltips used clear glass, which barely blurs what is behind it, so over the window history the rows beneath showed through the tooltip's own figures. The tooltips now use the same frosted glass as the panel.
