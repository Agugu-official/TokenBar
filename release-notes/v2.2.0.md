## Features

- **Releases are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.** [#445](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/445)

  macOS now opens a downloaded Syrtis without a Gatekeeper warning, so the Homebrew cask no longer removes the quarantine flag on install. Installed copies update through the in-app updater as before: in testing, an installed v2.1.0 updated itself to a Developer-ID build, and launch at login and quota reads were unaffected.

- **Each release has an installer DMG.** [#454](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/454)

  `Syrtis-2.2.0.dmg` on the release page opens a window with the app and an Applications folder to drag it onto. It is signed and notarized like the app. In-app updates and the Homebrew cask keep using `Syrtis.app.tar.gz`.

- **A new animated menu bar icon: Sand shoal.** [#441](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/441)

  Sand falls onto a dune while a current carries grains off its far side. It plays at a fixed 24 fps in four levels: as the live token rate passes 50K, 300K and 1.5M tokens per minute at the default pace, more sand falls and the dune grows and erodes further. Suggested on Discord by charliie.

- **Animated icons follow a new speed curve, set by an animation pace.** [#441](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/441)

  The cat and parrot run at 2 fps up to 50K tokens per minute, then speed up on a log curve to 40 fps at 3M. The previous curve was linear and reached full speed at 1M. A pace choice under Settings → Menubar icon scales this for how busy your agents are: Light reaches full speed at 600K tokens per minute, Moderate (the default) at 3M, and Heavy at about 10M. The same pace moves the Sand shoal's level thresholds.

- **First-run setup cards on the Overview.** [#442](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/442) — relates to [#435](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/issues/435)

  Cards at the top of the Overview cover which agents get a tab, the menu bar icon and text, the animation pace, usage attribution, starting at login, and the Discord profile. They show at once and each is answered on its own; "Skip setup" answers the rest. Existing users see them once too, with their current choices already selected. The Discord card replaces the Discord alert that appeared at launch, and neither of its buttons turns the Discord profile on.

## Fixes

- **With no quota reading, the menu bar gauge looked like a full allowance.** [#439](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/439) — fixes [#419](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/issues/419)

  When no reading is available, for example when every provider fails at once under Auto, the gauge is now drawn faded with a diagonal slash, in every gauge style and in the Settings preview.

- **An old reading's title kept its warning color.** [#440](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/440) — fixes [#420](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/issues/420)

  v2.1.0 turned the gauge grey once its reading was more than 30 minutes old, but the "Quota left" title beside it stayed red or amber, and with the cat or parrot nothing showed the reading was old. The title now turns the same grey, over both automatic and custom colors, and takes its color back with the next fresh reading.

- **Switching pages kept the previous scroll position.** [#444](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/444)

  A new Settings page, or a new tab or view in the dashboard, now starts at the top.

- **Two Settings hints described behavior the app does not have.** [#443](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/443)

  Data refresh said the live tokens-per-minute figure updates every few seconds whatever the setting, which is not how the app reads it; the hint now says the setting sets how often all logs are reread in full. Overview cards said the list sets their order; the order is fixed. The Settings hints, the usage attribution page, the Claude setup-token note and the Grok Bot Keychain notice were also rewritten in shorter, plainer language in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.
