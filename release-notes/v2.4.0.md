## Features

- **The window card can switch between accounts.** [#462](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/462)

  When an agent has two or more accounts with live windows, such as extra Claude config folders or captured Antigravity accounts, the window card on its Quota view shows a row of account buttons above the window buttons. Picking an account switches the card's windows, live bar, curve and window history to that account, and the choice is remembered for each agent. The main account's button shows its email when the account reports one, and a card showing another account names it right after the title. With a single account the card looks as before.

  Window names on the card are shorter, for example "Gemini · Weekly" instead of "Gemini Models · Weekly Limit Remaining", and the button rows wrap onto a second line when they do not fit.

- **Antigravity has a window card.** [#462](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/462)

  An agent that keeps no usage records on this Mac, such as Antigravity, now gets the window card and its window history on its tab. Its local usage area reads "Local usage can't be attributed to this account yet." When Antigravity's quota is read through agy and agy is signed in to a captured account, the main account's card shows that account's curve and history.

  Antigravity's weekly and 5-hour windows draw a curve from their first reading. Their length is now taken from what agy and Google report, instead of being learned over several resets.

## Fixes

- **With automatic capture off, capturing the account agy is using now merges it into one card.** [#467](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/467)

  Before, that account appeared twice: once as Antigravity's main card and once as its captured card. The merge lasts until agy next refreshes its sign-in, usually within an hour; with automatic capture on it stays merged, and Settings now says so while it is off. The hint asking you to capture only accounts other than agy's current one is gone.

- **The Antigravity card could stay on "not logged in" after a single slow agy run.** [#469](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/469)

  A timed-out `agy` run is now retried after 15 minutes, and meanwhile the card says "Antigravity CLI usage timed out. Retrying automatically." Whenever agy actually ran, its own error replaces "Antigravity is not logged in". Other agy failures still wait until agy's sign-in changes, as before.

- **Claude's Designs and Daily Routines windows would show an empty history in Anthropic's newer limits format.** [#466](https://github.com/Nanako0129/syrtis/pull/466)

  If Anthropic reports either weekly limit in its newer list of limits, its window history now counts your usage instead of matching none.
