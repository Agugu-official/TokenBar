Features:
- A card offers to attribute your usage to subscriptions. Until usage is attributed, a subscription's window history lists 0 tokens and $0.00 for every window. When nothing is attributed yet, a card at the top of the Overview tab and of the Quota view on every tab lists the suggested assignments and applies them in one click; "Set up manually…" opens Settings → Usage attribution, and "Not now" hides the card for good.
- Grok Build models are listed under one name. grok-4.6-build and grok-4.6 were two rows for one model; Models, Monthly, Daily and the Grok quota card now list grok-4.6 and grok-4.7 once each. Totals are unchanged.
- The menu bar gauge turns grey when its reading is more than 30 minutes old, with a quota window chosen explicitly in Settings. Auto mode is unchanged.

Fixes:
- Setting the clock back could erase quota history. Loading dropped a whole series when it held readings stamped later than the current time, and a poll that recorded anything then saved the loss. With the clock set back 10 minutes on a copy of a real history file, the previous version dropped 10,163 readings to 3,780 in memory; this version drops only the 4 readings stamped in the future. Repairs made while loading are now written back once.
- Model prices stopped updating while you were idle. The model report is now fetched again once it is an hour old.
- Without agy, every quota refresh started a login shell to look for it. It now runs at most once every 15 minutes.
- Claude credential errors now name their cause, and none includes credential content. A Keychain credential with a character outside printable ASCII is now read.
- A malformed Codex token could skip the refresh check; it is now rejected. Thanks @snowman12320.
- Quota history that failed to load was described as never recorded; it now says it could not be read and will be retried. A source that records no history, such as Antigravity read through agy, shows nothing recorded yet.
- On macOS 27 in light mode, the separators in the glass panel are lighter.
