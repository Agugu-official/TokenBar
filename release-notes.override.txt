Fixes:
- Codex quota windows that ended before their advertised reset were deleted instead of recorded. Codex moves its reported weekly reset ahead of schedule, and a window that ended early was neither complete nor the one in progress, so the next save dropped it. On one account's raw sample log the reset moved on 11.6% of consecutive readings, which is why Codex window history stayed near empty while other providers filled in normally. Such a window is now closed at the length it actually ran and kept.
- Windows lost before this update do not come back. Their samples were deleted at the time, so there is nothing left to rebuild from; the fix only stops it happening again.
- Windows recovered this way are shorter than a full week, because they ended when Codex reset rather than when the quota ran out. They appear in Window history, but they are left out of the run-out estimate, which still needs cycles that ran their full length. If you see the window count rise while the estimate stays unavailable, that is why.
