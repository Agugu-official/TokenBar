Fixes:
- Grok weekly usage now reads the whole weekly credit pool instead of the command-line tool's share of it, so an exhausted subscription no longer shows credit remaining. If you also use Grok outside the CLI, the weekly quota shown as remaining drops once after this update, and used rises to match — the old figure was overstating what was left, and nothing new was consumed.
- Grok weekly pace history starts over for the same reason. Samples recorded before this update measured the old per-product share, so keeping them would make run-out forecasts fit two different quantities. It rebuilds over the next few cycles; the monthly meter and your saved window selection are unchanged.
- Token counts just below a unit no longer render as "1000M" or "1000K". A count between 999.5M and 1B now reads 1B, and one between 999.5K and 1M reads 1M. Counts outside those two bands are unchanged.
