Fixes:
- Codex quota windows that ended before their advertised reset were deleted instead of recorded. Codex moves its reported weekly reset ahead of schedule, and a window that ended early was neither complete nor the one in progress, so the next save dropped it. On one account's raw sample log the reset moved on 11.6% of consecutive readings, which is why Codex window history stayed near empty while other providers filled in normally. Such a window is now closed at the length it actually ran, and appears in Window history like any other.

Three limits worth knowing:
- Closing a window does not lower the bar for recording one. It still needs readings spread from near its start to near its end without a long gap, so a window seen only in patches is dropped exactly as before. If TokenBar was not running for much of a window, that window is still lost.
- History lost before this update does not come back. Those samples were deleted at the time, so nothing remains to rebuild from.
- A window cut short by more than a tenth of its length stays out of the pace projection, since one that stopped at 34% used would teach the run-out forecast that a full cycle ends there. A window that still ran nine tenths of its length counts as finished and feeds the projection as before.
