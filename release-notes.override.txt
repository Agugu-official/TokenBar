Fixes:
- Antigravity quota keeps updating when the Antigravity IDE is closed, and now appears even when the IDE is not installed. TokenBar reads it from your signed-in Google account, or from the Antigravity command-line tool (agy) when that is what is available, instead of depending on the running IDE.
- Antigravity no longer shows "OAuth client was not found" on a Mac where the IDE is installed. Reading the sign-in client from the installed app could absorb a neighbouring value and reject a valid one, which silently disabled the account route whenever the IDE was closed.
