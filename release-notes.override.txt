New:
- TokenBar can publish what you are working on to Discord. It is off by default, and you choose whether it shows the agent, a cost band, both or neither, and which agent by name.

Improved:
- Opening the popover no longer waits for two extra scans. The chart runs on its own and appears as soon as it is ready.
- Scanning is faster: on an 8 GB history, a cold scan dropped about 31 percent and a warm scan about 37 percent. Peak memory is about 15 percent higher, which is the trade for the parallel parsing.
- Restarting shows your last dashboard immediately and refreshes behind it, instead of showing nothing for several seconds.
- Turns that an in-place transcript rewrite used to drop are recovered, so Claude history totals may be higher than before. That is the fix, not inflation.

Fixes:
- Inactive segments of the view switch are clickable again.
- Codex shows its monochrome mark instead of the ChatGPT green.
- Hovering a heatmap cell rings it, the way the bar chart already did.

Note: the first launch after this update rescans every client once, because the engine's cache format changed. Later launches are back to normal.
