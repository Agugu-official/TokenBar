Fixes:
- Codex quota windows that ended before their advertised reset were deleted instead of recorded. Codex moves its reported weekly reset ahead of schedule, and a window that ended early was neither complete nor the one in progress, so the next save dropped it. On one account's raw sample log the reset moved on 11.6% of consecutive readings, which is why Codex window history stayed near empty while other providers filled in normally. Such a window is now closed at the length it actually ran and kept, and it appears in Window history like any other.
- Closing a window does not lower the bar for recording one. It still needs enough readings, spread from near its start to near its end without a long gap in the middle, which means TokenBar has to have been running for most of it. A window observed only in patches is still dropped, exactly as before — closing changes when a window ends, not how thoroughly it was watched.
- Windows lost before this update do not come back. Their samples were deleted at the time, so there is nothing left to rebuild from; the fix only stops it happening again.
- A window cut short by more than a tenth of its length is left out of the pace projection — the deficit marker and the run-out forecast. It ended when Codex reset rather than when the quota ran out, so fitting one that stopped at 34% used would teach the projection that a completed cycle ends there and pull every later forecast low. A window that still ran at least nine tenths of its advertised length counts as having finished and feeds the projection as before. The exclusion applies to the projection only; every window kept this way is shown and counted everywhere else.
