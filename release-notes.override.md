## Fixes

- **Grok weekly usage read the CLI's share of the weekly credits rather than the credits themselves.** xAI reports two numbers: the shared weekly credit pool, and a split of that same pool across products — `GrokBuild`, `GrokChat`, and the rest. TokenBar preferred the `GrokBuild` row, which sits below the pool by whatever the other products spent, so anyone using Grok outside the command-line tool saw less depletion than they had. At an exhausted pool the card could still read 4% remaining while other trackers read zero. The pool percentage is now the only figure that answers the weekly window; a product row can still prove a week is not empty, but is never read as the value. Reported by [@terry-deephow](https://github.com/terry-deephow) with three trackers captured side by side, which is what made the disagreement measurable rather than a suspicion. [#240](https://github.com/Nanako0129/TokenBar/issues/240)

  Two things follow on the first refresh after updating. The weekly figure steps up once, by however much of your Grok usage happens outside the CLI — that is the correction landing, not new usage. And the weekly pace history starts over: every sample recorded before this release holds the old per-product share, so continuing that series would fit run-out forecasts across two different quantities and report a burn rate nothing consumed. It rebuilds over the next few cycles. The monthly meter and your saved window selection are untouched.

- **Token counts just below a unit rendered as "1000M" or "1000K".** The compact figure picked its unit from the raw count and only then rounded, so nothing re-checked the unit when the rounded number carried into the next one. Every value from 999,500,000 through 999,999,999 showed as `1000M` instead of `1B`, and 999,500 through 999,999 as `1000K` instead of `1M`. The menu-bar title recomputes at most every five minutes, so a count landing in one of those bands could hold the wrong string on screen for a full cycle. Unit selection now happens at the rounding boundary; every count outside the two affected bands renders exactly as before. Reported by [@charlychiu](https://github.com/charlychiu), who traced the affected range, the root cause and both candidate fixes from source, and flagged that the same string is part of a shared output contract with the Windows port — which moved in the same step. [#241](https://github.com/Nanako0129/TokenBar/issues/241)
